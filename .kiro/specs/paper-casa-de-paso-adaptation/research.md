# Research and Discovery: Adaptation of 'Casa de Paso'

## Journal Analysis: Revista Migraciones (IUEM)
- **Rank:** Scopus Q1 (Cultural Studies) / Q2 (Demography).
- **Style:** APA 7th Edition.
- **Length:** 7,000 - 9,000 words (including references).
- **Structure:** IMRaD (Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion).
- **Review:** Double-blind peer review.
- **Language:** Spanish (primary) and English.
- **Deadlines:** Call for papers active for "Political Ecology of Migration" (Sept 2025) and "Inequalities in Internationalization of Science" (Nov 2025).

## Context: Public Policies in Chile (2024-2025)
- **Policy:** Política Nacional de Migración y Extranjería (PNME) - Decree 181 (86 measures).
- **Focus:** Regularization for humanitarian reasons (women/children), intersectoral coordination for basic services.
- **Housing Deficit:** "Housing of transition" is a growing trend in Santiago (Estación Central, Santiago Centro).
- **Architectural Research:** Focus on "habitar digno" in camps (tomas) and architectural reception with an anthropological approach.

## Implementation Risks and Decisions
| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Word Count Overload** | Surgical editing to maintain density while adding critical analysis. |
| **Citation Consistency** | Cross-validation using `citation-management` and script `validate-citations.py`. |
| **Bias/Rigor** | Use `scientific-critical-thinking` skill to evaluate humanitarian vs. technical balance. |
| **Anonymization** | Ensure all metadata and direct mentions of UTEM/UW-Milwaukee are removed in the final manuscript for review. |

## Technology Alignment
- **Format:** Markdown (primary) -> LaTeX (via templates) -> PDF.
- **Citations:** BibTeX (`references/references.bib`) managed via APA style.
- **Scripts:** Usage of project-specific validation scripts (`validate-prose.py`, `validate-metadata.py`, etc.).
